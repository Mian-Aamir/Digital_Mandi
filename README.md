# 🌾 Digital_Mandi

## 📌 Project Overview

Digital_Mandi is a web-based platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, and suppliers directly. The goal is to eliminate middlemen (arti) and ensure fair pricing and transparency in agricultural trade.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

In Pakistan, farmers usually sell their crops through middlemen (arti), who take commission from both farmers and buyers. This reduces farmers' profit and increases costs for buyers.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Digital_Mandi provides a direct communication platform where:

* Farmers can list their crops
* Buyers can purchase directly from farmers
* Shopkeepers can list agricultural products (fertilizers, seeds, sprays)

This ensures:

* Better profit for farmers
* Fair prices for buyers
* Transparent system
