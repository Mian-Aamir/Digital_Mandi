How Digital Mandi Works

Simple 4 steps from listing to delivery

1

Register & Login

Sign up as a Farmer, Buyer, or Shopkeeper. Each role gets its own dashboard.

2

List Your Crops

Farmers add their crops with photos, quantity, location, and asking price.

3

Deal & Pay Securely

Buyer sends offer, farmer accepts. Payment goes into escrow until delivery.

4

Delivery & Payment Release

Delivery man delivers the crop. Payment releases to farmer after confirmation.
